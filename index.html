Grade Calculator

Use this free weighted grade calculator to calculate your overall subject percentage from assessment marks and weightages.

Enter the marks you earned, the maximum marks for each assessment, and its percentage weightage to calculate your final result.

This calculator supports up to six assessments and helps you work out your weighted average quickly.

More Details

My calculator uses a grade system, where 70% is Grade A, 60% is B, 50% (pass) is C, 40% is D and below is U (ungraded/fail).
